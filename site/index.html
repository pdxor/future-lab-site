Kalomo Sala Jordan

Visionary Artist · Sound & Art Installation (OM OLAK)

Read biography +

A visionary artist born in Middletown, CT in 1973, Kalomo Sala Jordan channels his visions into artwork drawn from a lifetime of exploring the psyche and multi-dimensional reality. His science-fiction sensibility blends the futuristic with the ancient — Egyptian, Aztec, and other cultures and planetary star systems that inform his influences. His connection to other realms shows through the information and alien languages transmitted in his work. Focusing on the emotion of love, he keeps his imaging clean and pure — line work and blended colors, often in UV paints that bring light out of the darkness of space and other realms, while inviting a calm, secure mind state in everyone who experiences it.

His mission on this planet is to help expand human consciousness as a whole while connecting with like-minded souls through his art in all its forms. A self-taught painter from an early age, his work has been shown throughout the Northeast, New York City, and as far as Tokyo, Japan. Beyond painting, Kalomo designs custom handmade jewelry, clothing, and footwear. For this next chapter his intention is to stay awake and aware — to paint with pure compassion and love, for the healing of all things that live, with infinite and eternal light. At Future Lab he presents a combined sound and art installation.

Under the name OM OLAK, Kalomo has been part of the psytrance scene since the late ’90s, based out of New England and New York City. He co-founded Awake Productions in 1996 and has spent three decades DJing, producing, and creating events under the names Meszenjah and OM OLAK — spanning psytrance and drum & bass, and helping pioneer New England’s electronic music scene. He has opened for acts like Infected Mushroom and shared bills with Mubali, Kox Box, and Kabayun, and worked alongside the Fractal Tribe collective for over a decade. Now a member of Goajira Records (Philadelphia), he pushes twilight, forest, dark, and high-tech psytrance — dark psy and forest from 145 to 160 bpm, with hard kicks, supersonic basslines, and galactic melodies that build the mothership through sound. Trance to make them dance.

Greg DeCarlo

AI Innovator · Software Architect · Immersive Tech

Read biography +

Greg DeCarlo is an AI innovator, software architect, and creator of immersive tech. He’s passionate about transforming how businesses and individuals harness artificial intelligence to solve real-world challenges. With over 20 years at the intersection of enterprise architecture and emerging technologies, he builds bridges between complex business needs and elegant technical solutions.

What sets his approach apart is a focus on making advanced AI accessible and practical — whether modernizing legacy systems, creating intuitive AI assistants, or developing cloud-native applications that scale. His recent work centers on platforms like Cloud Nine AI (open-source) and iSaid.ai, technologies that empower users to build powerful AI applications without deep technical expertise. He believes the next wave of innovation will come from putting AI tools in the hands of domain experts who understand the problems that need solving.

Marc Pettersen

Creative Technologist · Educator · Community Builder

Read biography +

Marc Pettersen is a creative technologist, educator, and community builder working at the intersection of art, technology, storytelling, and hands-on making. Through his work with Hartford Public Library’s YOUmedia and his emerging project, Pixel & Palette, he explores how tools like 3D printing, animation, game design, mixed reality, and immersive technology can bring people together — not simply to experience something, but to create it.
